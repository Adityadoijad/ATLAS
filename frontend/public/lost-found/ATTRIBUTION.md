# Lost & Found demo item photos

These four photographs illustrate the **seeded demo reports** on the Lost & Found
board. They are representative images of that *kind* of item — not photographs of
any actual lost property — and the UI labels them "Representative image" so nobody
mistakes one for the item they are looking for.

Reports submitted through the app carry the reporter's own photo instead, or no
photo at all. A stand-in picture is never substituted for a real report.

All four are from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licences that permit
redistribution with attribution. The credit is shown on each card in the app and
must not be removed.
